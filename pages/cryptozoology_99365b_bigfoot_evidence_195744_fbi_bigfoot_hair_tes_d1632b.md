## Introduction

Among the many pieces of evidence cited by Bigfoot enthusiasts, few have attracted as much attention as the FBI’s “Bigfoot file”. Because the file comes from a major federal law-enforcement agency, it is sometimes presented as [proof]({{ 'proof/' | relative_url }}) that the government took the possibility of Bigfoot seriously. The documents tell a more limited story. In the late 1970s, the FBI agreed to analyse a small collection of hairs and skin that a Bigfoot researcher believed might come from an unknown creature. After laboratory examination, the bureau concluded that the hairs were not from an undiscovered primate at all. They were identified as originating from the deer family. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://vault.fbi.gov/bigfoot/Bigfoot%20Part%2001%20%28Final%29/at_download/file" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: vault.fbi.gov">[FBI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">vault.fbi.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Information15 Dec 1976 — of hairs by the FBI Laboratory in connection with the. Bigfoot phenomenon... Sasquatch hair was analyzed by...</span></span></span>

For scientists evaluating Bigfoot claims, the episode is noteworthy not because it validated the legend, but because it illustrates a recurring pattern in [cryptozoology]({{ 'cryptozoology/' | relative_url }}): biological evidence that appears promising often turns out to belong to known animals when subjected to formal testing. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.com/articles/bigfoot-fbi-file-investigation-discovery" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.com">[HISTORY]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bigfoot Was Investigated by the FBI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Here&#x27;s What They Found6 Jun 2019 — The FBI released its four-decade-old file on Bigfoot including an analysis conducted on hair that alle...</span></span></span>

## Why the Sample Reached the FBI

The case began with Peter Byrne, one of the most prominent Bigfoot investigators of the 1970s and director of the Oregon-based Bigfoot Information Center and Exhibition. Byrne contacted the FBI in 1976 after encountering references suggesting that the bureau might previously have examined alleged Bigfoot material. FBI officials replied that they could find no record of such earlier examinations. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://abc13.com/post/fbi-releases-documents-from-1977-bigfoot-investigation/5332943/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: abc13.com">[ABC13 Houston]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">abc13.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">fbi releases documents from 1977 bigfoot investigation</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ABC13 HoustonFBI releases documents from Bigfoot investigation, finding...Jun 5, 2019 — The FBI investigated hair and skin in the 1970s...</span></span></span>

Byrne then asked whether the bureau would analyse a specimen in his possession: roughly fifteen hairs attached to a small piece of skin. He considered the sample unusually important, describing it as the first material in several years that seemed potentially significant to his investigation. The request was supported by publicity surrounding Bigfoot research and by Byrne’s association with the Academy of Applied Science, an organisation that had sponsored expeditions into other unexplained-animal claims. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://abc13.com/post/fbi-releases-documents-from-1977-bigfoot-investigation/5332943/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: abc13.com">[ABC13 Houston+2Newsweek]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">abc13.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">fbi releases documents from 1977 bigfoot investigation</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">ABC13 HoustonFBI releases documents from Bigfoot investigation, finding...Jun 5, 2019 — The FBI investigated hair and skin in the 1970s...</span></span></span>

Although FBI laboratories normally focused on criminal investigations, Assistant Director Jay Cochran Jr. authorised an exception. Internal correspondence stated that the examination would be conducted in the interest of research and scientific inquiry rather than as part of a criminal case. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.cbsnews.com/sacramento/news/fbi-investigated-bigfoot-in-the-1970s-documents-reveal/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: cbsnews.com">[CBS News]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">cbsnews.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">fbi investigated bigfoot in the 1970s documents reveal</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">CBS NewsFBI Investigated Bigfoot In The 1970s, Documents RevealJun 5, 2019 — The FBI analyzed a sample of alleged Bigfoot hairs in the 19...</span></span></span>

## What the Hair Result Said

The FBI laboratory examined the submitted hairs during late 1976 and early 1977. The final response was brief and direct. According to the bureau’s findings, the hairs were identified as being of “deer family origin”. In zoological terms, that means they belonged to a member of the family Cervidae, which includes deer, elk, moose and related species. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.avclub.com/the-fbi-just-released-its-bigfoot-file-1835277774" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: avclub.com">[AV Club+2FBI]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">avclub.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AV ClubThe FBI just released its Bigfoot fileWhile the FBI denied having tested Bigfoot hair in the past, they were, as it turned out, mo...</span></span></span>

The result was not ambiguous. The laboratory did not report an unknown species, an unclassifiable mammal or evidence consistent with a hidden North American ape. Instead, the sample was attributed to a familiar group of animals already common across the regions where many Bigfoot reports occur. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.history.com/articles/bigfoot-fbi-file-investigation-discovery" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: history.com">[HISTORY]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">history.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Bigfoot Was Investigated by the FBI</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Here&#x27;s What They Found6 Jun 2019 — The FBI released its four-decade-old file on Bigfoot including an analysis conducted on hair that alle...</span></span></span>

The FBI subsequently returned the material to Byrne. The correspondence preserved in the released file contains no indication that further testing uncovered anything unusual or that the bureau considered the specimen evidence for Bigfoot. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.avclub.com/the-fbi-just-released-its-bigfoot-file-1835277774" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: avclub.com">[AV Club]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">avclub.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">AV ClubThe FBI just released its Bigfoot fileWhile the FBI denied having tested Bigfoot hair in the past, they were, as it turned out, mo...</span></span></span>

## What the Released FBI File Actually Shows

When the FBI publicly released the file decades later through its records vault, [media]({{ 'media/' | relative_url }}) coverage often emphasised the novelty of a federal “Bigfoot investigation”. The documents themselves reveal something narrower.

The file primarily consists of correspondence between Byrne and FBI officials, logistical discussions about testing, and the laboratory result. It does not document a broad government hunt for Bigfoot, a secret programme, or evidence that the bureau believed an unknown primate existed. Instead, it records a single examination of a submitted specimen. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://vault.fbi.gov/bigfoot/Bigfoot%20Part%2001%20%28Final%29/at_download/file" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: vault.fbi.gov">[FBI+2Live Science]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">vault.fbi.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Information15 Dec 1976 — of hairs by the FBI Laboratory in connection with the. Bigfoot phenomenon... Sasquatch hair was analyzed by...</span></span></span>

This distinction matters because the existence of an FBI file can sound more dramatic than the contents actually are. The file demonstrates that a government laboratory was willing to evaluate a physical sample. It does not demonstrate that the sample supported the Bigfoot hypothesis. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.popularmechanics.com/science/animals/a71064345/fbi-official-bigfoot-file-released/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: popularmechanics.com">[Popular Mechanics]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">popularmechanics.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Popular Mechanics FBI Released Bigfoot&#x27;s Official File</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Popular MechanicsFBI Released Bigfoot&#x27;s Official File - Is Bigfoot Real?19 Apr 2026 — The FBI&#x27;s FOIA Vault features a revealing 22-page B...</span></span></span>

## Why Official Testing Did Not Validate Bigfoot

From a scientific perspective, the significance of the case lies in its outcome rather than in the fact that testing occurred. Claims about undiscovered animals become stronger when physical evidence survives independent examination. In this instance, one of the more promising [specimens]({{ 'specimens/' | relative_url }}) available to investigators produced a conventional explanation. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.inverse.com/article/56478-did-peter-byrne-really-find-bigfoot-hair-and-skin" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: inverse.com">[Inverse]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">inverse.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Set the record straight, once and for all.&quot; by Peter Hess.
